Legal Landscape for Malaysia Online Gambling

Like we have already stated earlier on in this review, Malaysia’s online gambling laws are built on outdated frameworks. The Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 prohibits all forms of unlicensed gambling, including online, but it is an old law introduced before the internet. However, this law does not explicitly address offshore digital casinos.

A key development occurred in September 2023 when the Court of Appeal ruled that Malaysian authorities can block and investigate unauthorized gambling sites, even if those sites are located overseas. However, the enforcement mainly targets operators, not individual players. So, players can continue using online casinos for as long as they are cautious.

Fortunately, the government is expected to hold public consultations for a new Digital Gambling Regulation Bill aimed at updating the current laws to better reflect today’s online environment by the end of 2025.
