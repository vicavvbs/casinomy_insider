Responsible Gambling

We are committed to promoting safe, controlled, and responsible gambling. While online casino entertainment can be exciting, it’s important to approach it with awareness and balance.

Our Commitment

We believe that gambling should be fun—not a source of financial stress or harm. That’s why we:

  • Promote casinos that offer self-exclusion tools, deposit and loss limits, and session reminders
  • Highlight wagering requirements and risk in every bonus review
  • Encourage users to play within their means and set personal limits
  • Reject promotions that target vulnerable individuals or glamorize high-risk behavior

We actively avoid language that suggests guaranteed wins or risk-free gambling.

Know the Signs of Problem Gambling

Gambling becomes a problem when it interferes with your finances, work, relationships, or mental well-being. Some warning signs include:

  • Chasing losses or gambling with money you can’t afford to lose
  • Hiding gambling behavior from friends or family
  • Feeling anxious, depressed, or irritable when not gambling
  • Using gambling as a way to escape stress or financial hardship

If you or someone you know may be struggling, it’s important to seek help early.

Tools & Resources You Can Use

Most licensed online casinos offer tools that help you stay in control:

  • Deposit Limits – Cap how much you can deposit daily, weekly, or monthly
  • Loss Limits – Set the maximum amount you’re willing to lose
  • Reality Checks – Get reminders on how long you’ve been playing
  • Time-Outs & Self-Exclusion – Temporarily or permanently block access to your account

We test these tools during every casino review and only recommend sites that implement them properly.

Help & Support Services

If you need help managing your gambling habits, contact one of the following organizations:

  • GambleAware – www.gambleaware.org
  • Gamblers Anonymous – www.gamblersanonymous.org
  • National Council on Problem Gambling (Singapore) – www.ncpg.org.sg

All of these organizations offer free, confidential assistance.
